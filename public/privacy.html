Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how Alizson Cakes & Snacks ("Alizson", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use our website to order baked goods and baking supplies. We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. Who we are

Alizson Cakes & Snacks, Obasanjo Hilltop, Adefunke Shodunke Street, Abeokuta, Ogun State, Nigeria.
Email: alizsoncakes2@gmail.com · Phone/WhatsApp: +234 703 014 4746

2. What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address and phone number. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Order details — items ordered, delivery address, Local Government Area, delivery time slot, gift messages and special instructions.
  • Location — the map location you choose for delivery, so riders can find you. Our delivery riders share their live location with us while delivering your order.
  • Payment information — card payments are processed by Paystack. We receive a payment reference and status, but we never see or store your card details. For bank transfers we record that your transfer was confirmed.
  • Feedback — reviews, ratings and messages you send us.
  • Device storage — your browser stores your cart, wishlist and display preferences, and keeps you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How we use it

  • To take, prepare, deliver and track your orders (performance of our contract with you).
  • To confirm payments and prevent fraud (our legitimate interests and legal obligations).
  • To contact you about your order by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To keep records required for accounting and tax purposes (legal obligation).
  • To improve our products and service using your feedback.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not send marketing messages without your consent.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what each party needs to do its job:

  • Our delivery riders — your name, phone number, delivery address and location for orders they deliver.
  • Paystack — to process card payments.
  • Supabase — hosts our database and sign-in system.
  • Vercel — hosts our website.
  • Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google" to sign in.
  • Authorities — where the law requires it.

Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards permitted under the NDPA.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account details while your account is active. We keep order and payment records for as long as Nigerian accounting and tax law requires (generally six years). Rider location data for a delivery is kept only as long as needed to complete and resolve that delivery.

6. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, where we are not required to keep it;
  • stop or limit certain uses of your data, or withdraw consent you have given.

Email alizsoncakes2@gmail.com to make a request. We will respond within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Access to customer data is restricted by account role, so customers see only their own orders and riders see only the deliveries assigned to them.

8. Children

Our services are intended for people aged 18 and over. Under-18s should order with a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
